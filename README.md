# poolpulse
Privacy-first lane busyness for swimming pools in UK and EU (pilot: Edinburgh, Glasgow and Leiden)

**Pilot pools:** Pools in Edinburgh, Glasgow and Leiden
**Lane model:** Fast / Medium / Slow  
**Signals:** Verified check-ins (OTP for contributors), aggregated and auto-expiring.

## Why
Lane crowding is one of the most frustrating parts of pool swimming. PoolPulse helps swimmers decide **when to go** and **which lane type to choose**.

## Privacy
No cameras. No surveillance. No location tracking. Aggregated busyness only.  
See `docs/PRIVACY.md`.
